My Reflection on AI Literacy

After completing these nine missions, I understood the basic concepts of AI and how it is used in daily life. I learned the differences between machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI. I also understood how LLMs work and why their answers should be verified using reliable sources.

As a VLSI Physical Design learner, I think AI can help me understand technical concepts, analyze timing reports, and learn scripting. However, I should not depend on AI completely. I need to improve my own technical knowledge and check AI-generated results before using them.

Overall, these missions helped me understand how to use AI effectively and responsibly in my studies and future career.
